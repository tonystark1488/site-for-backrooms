Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Application handles data collection and protection.

1. Data Collection

No Personal Information: We do not collect, store, or process any personal data such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical location.

Blockchain Data: Because this application interacts with the Solana blockchain, your public wallet address and transaction history are publicly available on the network. We do not store this data on private servers.

Camera Features: If the application requires camera permissions for "proof of reality" mechanics, images are processed locally on your device or minted directly onto the blockchain. We do not upload or store your images on external servers.

2. Data Protection & Security

Since we do not collect personal information, we do not store your data. Your on-chain interactions are secured by the cryptography of the Solana network and your own chosen wallet software.

3. Third-Party Services

The Application may use decentralized storage systems (like IPFS or Arweave) or RPC nodes to interact with the Solana network. These independent services handle open on-chain data and do not have access to your private personal information.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
